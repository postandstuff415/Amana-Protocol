# Amana Protocol: The "Everyone Eats" Escrow Protocol
*Zero-Trust B2B Trade for Emerging Markets via USSD & Stablecoins*

## The Problem
Merchants in African trade corridors (Kenya, Uganda, Ghana) face a "Trust Deficit" that stifles growth. Regional trade is plagued by three core frictions:
1. **Counterparty Risk**: Wholesalers demand upfront payment; retailers fear non-delivery.
2. **Predatory Fees**: Bank wires cost 6-10% in FX spreads and transaction fees.
3. **FX Scarcity**: Chronic US Dollar shortages delay cross-border settlements for weeks.

## The Solution: Amana Protocol
Amana is a zero-trust escrow layer that bypasses the legacy banking system using standard GSM feature phones.
- **USSD Interface**: No smartphone or internet required. Accessible to 100% of regional traders via simple text menus.
- **Stablecoin Settlement**: Instant settlement in USDC/USDT on Layer 2 (Celo/Base), eliminating FX delays and volatility.
- **Programmatic Escrow**: Funds are locked on-chain and only released upon verifiable delivery or SIM-authenticated confirmation.
- **Account Abstraction**: ERC-4337 wallets mapped to SIM cards, with gas fees sponsored by the protocol for a seamless user experience.

## Market Focus: The B2B Trade Corridors
We focus on high-volume B2B wholesalers and SMEs in the initial corridors:
- **Kenya ↔ Uganda**: $500M+ annual cross-border trade.
- **Kenya ↔ Ghana**: Emerging West-East trade link.
- **Phase 1 Target**: 500 pre-vetted merchants operating on feature phones.

## Revenue Model
The protocol generates sustainable revenue through two primary streams:
1. **1.0% FX Spread**: A transparent fee on every trade settled via the protocol.
2. **4.5% Annualized Yield**: Earned on escrowed stablecoin float via automated treasury sweeps into tokenized treasury bills.

## The "Everyone Eats" Model
