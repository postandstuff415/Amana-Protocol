# Amana Protocol: The "Everyone Eats" Escrow Protocol
*Zero-Trust B2B Trade for Emerging Markets via USSD & Stablecoins*

## The Problem
Merchants in African trade corridors (Kenya, Uganda, Ghana) face a "Trust Deficit" that stifles growth. Regional trade is plagued by:
1. **Counterparty Risk**: Wholesalers demand upfront payment; retailers fear non-delivery.
2. **Predatory Fees**: Bank wires cost 6-10% in FX spreads and transaction fees.
3. **FX Scarcity**: Chronic US Dollar shortages delay cross-border settlements for weeks.

## The Solution: Amana Protocol
Amana is a zero-trust escrow layer that bypasses the legacy banking system using standard GSM feature phones.
- **USSD Interface**: No smartphone or internet required. Accessible to 100% of regional traders.
- **Stablecoin Settlement**: Instant settlement in USDC/USDT, eliminating FX delays and volatility.
- **Programmatic Escrow**: Funds are locked on-chain and only released upon verifiable delivery or SIM-authenticated confirmation.
- **Account Abstraction**: ERC-4337 wallets mapped to SIM cards, with gas fees sponsored by the protocol for a seamless user experience.

## The "Everyone Eats" Model
Amana aligns incentives by programmatically distributing 100% of protocol revenue:
- **35% Front-End**: Merchant rebates and market ambassador incentives.
- **40% Middle-End**: Engineering, security audits, and telecom routing costs.
- **25% Back-End**: Sovereign float reserve and a 10% uncapped founder royalty.
